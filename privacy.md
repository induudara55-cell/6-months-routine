 # Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

## Introduction

Life Tracker ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

**We do NOT collect any personal data on our servers.** All your data is stored:

- **Locally** on your device using browser localStorage
- **In YOUR own Google Drive** (only if you enable cloud backup)

## Google Authentication

When you sign in with Google, we receive:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your profile picture

We use this information only to identify you within the app. We do NOT store this on any external server.

## Google Drive Backup

If you enable cloud backup, your app data is stored in a file called `life-tracker-backup.json` in your own Google Drive. We have no access to this file — only you do.

## Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and in your own Google Drive. We do not have access to your personal information.

## Third-Party Services

We use:
- **Google OAuth 2.0** for authentication
- **Google Drive API** for cloud backup
- **GitHub Pages** for hosting

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
