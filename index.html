User Guide

Learn how to use this app step by step. 👑

Water Tracker

Go to Sidebar → Water Tracker. Tap the glass icons or "Add 1 Glass". Goal is 8 per day.

Sleep Tracker

Enter bedtime and wake time, save. Add a quality rating with the stars.

Weight Log

Add your weight. See progress on the chart. Set a goal weight if you want.

Notes

Click "New Note". You can search, sort, and edit notes anytime.

AI Coach

Ask questions about daily life, habits, focus, motivation. Works in English and Sinhala.

Motivation

Watch speeches, listen to songs, and get inspired when you feel low.

Daily Routine

Edit any schedule item with the pencil icon. Toggle habits, use the Pomodoro timer.

Backup

Sign in with Google, then "Backup Now". Enable auto-backup for safe syncing every 5 minutes.
